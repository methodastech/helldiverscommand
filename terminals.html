The terminals

Eleven mission terminals rebuilt from the real step lists, with the real input beeps. Pick an objective and run it: W A S D to interact, exactly as the prompt in the game.

Sequences, pipe grids and security patterns are randomised each run, exactly as the terminals in the field. Arrow keys work too. Enter confirms, Escape aborts.

On this site the interact key is not needed. W A S D and the arrow keys work as in the field, Enter also confirms, Escape aborts.

Step lists and screen wording are the wiki's. The pipe and security-grid column controls follow the wiki and the published guides (A D pick a column, W S move it, locked columns stay). The sub-system switch names and the Hellbomb timer are reconstructed from screenshots. Waits run in real time at the wiki's durations. Enemy pressure and the sprint meter are training aids, not game mechanics, and are off by default.
