6 / Small print

Sources and disclaimer

Where the numbers come from

Stratagem codes, weapon statistics, enemy rosters and booster effects are compiled from the community-maintained Helldivers wiki, which mirrors the game's own data files. Live war telemetry comes from the open community war API that reads Arrowhead's public war endpoints.

Tier ratings, faction scores, loadouts and doctrine are editorial. They reflect the September 2026 consensus for Difficulty 8 to 10 play and they will drift as patches land. Treat them as a starting point, not an authority.
