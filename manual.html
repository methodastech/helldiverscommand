Four currencies

Super Credits

Buy Warbonds and Superstore armour. Found in the small grey supply containers and bunkers at Points of Interest (usually 10 at a time, occasionally 100), included as rewards inside every Warbond, or bought outright. Pickups are shared with every diver present and do not need extraction. No known cap.

Farm: Trivial or Easy, sweep every POI, extract, repeatShared on pickup

Medals

Unlock Warbond items. Earned per mission (more for completing an operation in sequence), from Personal and Major Orders, and from POI containers. Cap: 250. At the cap the game keeps showing rewards but pays zero, so spend before a Major Order lands.

Cap 250Spend before the MO pays out

Requisition Slips

Buy Stratagems and Ship Modules. From objectives, POI containers, and a mission payout multiplied by difficulty with bonuses for extracted divers and time remaining. Killing civilians on evacuation missions costs 25 each. Cap: 50,000.

Cap 50,000

Samples

Common, Rare and Super (Super Uranium). Carried physically, dropped on death, and only banked if they reach the extraction shuttle. Anyone can pick up a dropped batch. Drop them at the extraction zone if you pass through it early.

    7

    Mission rules that get divers killed

    EXTRACTION

    Two minutes, then twenty seconds

    Calling extraction starts a two minute timer and draws every patrol nearby. Once any diver boards, Pelican-1 lifts off after 20 seconds. If the whole squad leaves the zone as it arrives, it hovers and fires until someone steps back in. It can be shot down, and heavy strikes make it lift off. You can still extract after the mission timer ends, without stratagems.

    REINFORCEMENT

    Five per diver, regenerating

    Reinforce is UP DOWN RIGHT LEFT UP. The budget is five per person and refills one every two minutes once depleted. Boarding Pelican-1 clears every remaining ticket. A Hellpod is also a weapon: land it on a Charger, a Hulk or a fabricator.

    STRATAGEM JAMMER

    150 metres of silence

    Inside the radius no stratagem works, including objective stratagems, except SEAF Artillery. Disable it at the terminal in the base, then Hellbomb it, or drop a 500kg or a barrage on the dish once it is off.

    HELLBOMB

    Six inputs, ten seconds, forty-five metres

    Manually armed with a six input code. Kill radius 25 metres through walls; stand 45 metres away or go prone. Damaged Hellbombs found in the field detonate three seconds after small arms fire and instantly from explosives.

    SAMPLES

    They are not yours until you extract

    Dropped on death, banked only if a diver carries them onto the shuttle. Drop them at the extraction zone early if the route passes through it. Rare samples appear from Challenging, Super from Suicide Mission.

    DIFFICULTY

    Unlock the next by finishing this one

    Completing a full operation unlocks the next difficulty. Completing a harder operation you were carried into unlocks only the difficulty after it. Super Helldive is a different game: uptime and discipline, not power.
