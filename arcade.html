        Drill 6 · Helldive Simulator

        Helldive Simulator

        In the game · measured throw physics and fuses

        Throw grenades and stratagem beacons at bug holes and a moving Charger, then switch to Aim and shoot steel plates out to 150 m with real projectile drop. 3D, with the game's own controls.

        How the range works, in the game's numbers

        Reinforce, Resupply, SOS Beacon and the Hellbomb are always in the list and never take a slot. Hold Ctrl and the stratagem list appears; input the code with W A S D; release, and the beacon is in your hand: left click throws it. G throws your grenade, Shift sprints, Space dives, Q marks a distance. There is no charge: how far it flies is how high you aim, about 40 metres at 45 degrees, 34.6 at 30 or 60, 20 at 15 or 75; 65 metres at 45 in Servo-Assisted armour; a dive adds about 5 metres and a sprint dive about 15. Fuses and radii are the wiki's. A stratagem strike lands its call-in time after the beacon anchors.
