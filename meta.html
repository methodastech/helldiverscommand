How the score works

Every weapon is scored out of 1000 against its own slot, so a sidearm is judged as a sidearm. Each component is scaled across the weapons in that slot, then weighted. Hover any tile for its breakdown; the parts add up to the total.

Tiers are percentile cuts inside each slot, not fixed scores. The top 10 percent of a slot are S, the next 25 A, the next 35 B, the next 20 C, and the bottom 10 D, so a weapon is ranked against the weapons it competes with. That puts the S line at 594 for primaries, 714 for sidearms, 810 for support and 879 for grenades. Where two published community lists agree on a weapon, the model is blended 70 / 30 with that consensus: 43 weapons are, and their breakdown shows both the model subtotal and the blended score. Nine melee weapons are left unranked because reach, exposure and swing arcs are not modelled. Stats are the game's; the weights and the blend are ours.
